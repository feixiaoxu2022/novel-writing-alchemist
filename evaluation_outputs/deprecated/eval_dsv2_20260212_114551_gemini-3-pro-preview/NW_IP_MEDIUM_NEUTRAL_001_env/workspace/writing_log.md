### 4. 剧情进度摘要
- **Ch 1-5**: John arrives, saves Liuzi. Finds clue about Arthur. Rejects Huang's bribe.
- **Ch 6**: John robs the horse-drawn train. Discovers Huang is supplied by Cornwall/Pinkertons. Burns the opium, distributes the silver. Milton confirms John's identity.
- **Ch 7**: Milton confronts Huang, asserts dominance with intel. Tang is terrified of Pinkertons. John refuses to run, plans to visit the Madam's Tomb.
- **Ch 8**: Ambush at the Madam's Tomb. Milton uses a Maxim gun. John, Liuzi, and Tang are pinned. John uses Dead Eye to disable the gunner and the weapon, suffering severe physical backlash. They escape into the bamboo forest.
- **Current Goal**: Regroup in the bamboo forest, assess injuries, and plan the next move (Chapter 9).
